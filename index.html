RASED

An eye that sees, intelligence that understands, a system that responds. AI crowd safety and intelligent response platform, built by a team of five.

Expos bring thousands of visitors across many zones, but cameras, sensors and teams usually work in separate systems with no single operational picture, so incidents are spotted late and are hard to document. RASED brings cameras, microphones, environmental sensors and AI into one platform: it detects an incident the moment it happens, alerts the right team immediately with a snapshot, and documents it in a report ready for review.

Movement and emergencies, through cameras and audio

  • Sudden crowding and crowd density
  • Falls
  • Weapons and violence
  • Gunshots and explosions

Indoor environment and safety, through sensors

  • Fire and smoke indicators
  • Abnormal heat
  • Automatic ventilation

The alert journey

DetectCameras and sensors capture the event
AnalyseAI identifies the type and severity
AlertThe right team gets it with a snapshot
RespondThe team handles it on site
DocumentLogged with an editable draft report
ReviewReport checked before approval

Four layers, one system

01 Inputs
Live video and audio from cameras and microphones; temperature, humidity and pressure sensors
02 Analysis
AI engine (YOLO, VideoMAE, AST) for computer vision and audio; a local control node that runs fans when heat crosses a threshold
03 Platform
RASED server (FastAPI) that collects, manages and stores events and readings in one place
04 Outputs
Unified real-time React dashboard, instant Telegram alerts with the image, and draft reports ready for review

My role

  • Owned the cyber-physical (IoT/OT) layer: the ESP32 sensor nodes that trigger ventilation, and their FastAPI backend.
  • Secured it against fake or tampered sensor data: hashed per-node credentials, TLS, heartbeat tracking and cross-node validation.
  • Made it keep working through network loss, then tested access control on the telemetry and control APIs.
Full technical detail
  • Built the CPS node: an ESP32 reading a BME280 over I²C and driving 12V ventilation fans through a relay on a temperature threshold.
  • Built the CPS backend and logic in Python/FastAPI with SQLAlchemy/SQLite: REST APIs for readings, heartbeats and node management, surfaced in the platform dashboard.
  • Protected reading integrity against fabricated or modified data: per-node credentials stored as hashes, requests bound to node identity over TLS, heartbeat/last-seen tracking and cross-node validation to catch inconsistent readings.
  • Added offline resilience: nodes buffer readings during network loss and forward them on reconnect (store-and-forward).
  • HTTPS/TLS and API access-control testing on telemetry and control endpoints.
  • IoT/OT monitoring dashboards and a Mini Network Operations Center linking sensor data to response workflows.
